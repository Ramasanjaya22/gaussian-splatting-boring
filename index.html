How this is checkable

  • Enforced, not promised. connect-src 'self' in this page's CSP means the browser itself blocks any attempt to phone home. A leak would surface as a CSP violation in the console.
  • Self-hosted model. MediaPipe's WASM and the face model are served from this origin. Google is never contacted, so the "usage metrics" clause in their privacy notice has nowhere to send anything.
  • Trivially lossy. The renderer receives a 6-float head pose and nothing else. You cannot reconstruct a face from the pose of a face.
  • Cut the wire. The button below hard-fails every network API. The effect keeps working. That is the falsifiable version of the claim.
